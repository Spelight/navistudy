# Design System

## Direction

Extend the DXIR Words mini program's dark study console into a Military Theory companion. Preserve the compact navigation, visible progress, direct actions, and restrained blue status language while replacing vocabulary-specific patterns with question-type controls.

## Structure

- Three tabs: Practice, Question Bank, My Data.
- Wrong answers remain a focused secondary page reachable from Practice and My Data.
- Practice is one-question-at-a-time with an optional question palette.
- Stable answer rows use letter markers and explicit correct, incorrect, and selected states.

## Palette

- Canvas: near-black `#050608`.
- Panels: neutral charcoal `#1c1d20`.
- Primary and current state: cobalt `#2c7be5`.
- Correct: teal `#22b789`.
- Wrong: vermilion `#e05670`.
- Favorite: amber `#d79a27`.

## Voice

Direct, compact, and careful about source age. Avoid competitive rankings and unsupported claims.
