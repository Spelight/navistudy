# Product

## Platform

WeChat Mini Program using native WXML, WXSS, and JavaScript.

## Purpose

Turn the existing Military Theory question bank into short, resumable practice sessions on a phone. The mini program works offline after installation, stores progress locally, and can optionally sync Military Theory data through the shared NaviStudy passport username.

## Users

University students reviewing Military Theory coursework, especially during fragmented study time before an exam.

## Core Flows

- Select a chapter and begin sequential or random practice.
- Answer single-choice, multiple-choice, true/false, and short-answer questions.
- Resume the exact question from the last session.
- Review wrong answers and favorites.
- Search and browse the full question bank.
- Export or import local learning data through the clipboard.
- Pull or push Military Theory records across the web and mini-program clients without mixing NaviWords data.

## Content Boundary

The bank is compiled from local historical and current review materials. Historical questions are practice references only; current course materials and teacher guidance take precedence.
